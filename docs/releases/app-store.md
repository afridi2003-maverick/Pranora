## 2. Veterinary & Medical Disclaimer (Guideline 1.4.1 Compliance)
In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 1.4.1 (Medical/Health Advice) and Pranora REL-5 Veterinary Compliance:
> *"Pranora provides directory information for registered veterinary clinics and facilitates community animal welfare reporting. Any guidance provided by the AI Assistant is for informational reference only and does not substitute professional veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or clinical consultation. In life-threatening emergencies, users are instructed to proceed directly to the nearest emergency veterinary facility."*

This disclaimer is displayed:
1. Within the app terms (`/terms`)
2. At the launch of the AI Assistant (`/assistant`)
3. Directly beneath medical prescription views (`/medical`)
