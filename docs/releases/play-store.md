## 3. Sensitive Permissions Justification (Play Console Declaration)
1. **`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`**:
   - *Core purpose:* Required to sort nearest veterinary clinics by distance and pinpoint exact GPS location when reporting injured rescue animals.
2. **`CAMERA` & `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`**:
   - *Core purpose:* Required for users and rescuers to snap photos of distressed animals, upload vaccination cards, and update pet profile photos.
3. **`RECORD_AUDIO`**:
   - *Core purpose:* Required exclusively for the Push-to-Talk AI Voice Assistant feature.
4. **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**:
   - *Core purpose:* Critical delivery of time-sensitive vaccine reminders, appointment confirmations, and emergency rescue assignments.
